## Overview

Comparato is an Australian, privately held technology-enabled company building a personalised private health insurance comparison and switching service for eligible Australian consumers. Comparato gives Australians access to 25 leading private health insurance funds through a single, free, advisor-led conversation.

The company sits at the intersection of financial services, health, and consumer advocacy, with a strong focus on transparency, personalised advice, and compliance. Comparato enables Australians to navigate the complexity of the private health insurance market — comparing policies, understanding their coverage entitlements, and switching funds where appropriate — without paying a cent for the service and without receiving advice influenced by commercial incentives.

Founded in 2023 and commercially active shortly thereafter, Comparato's early traction has been built on a premium service model differentiated by human expertise rather than algorithmic comparison tools. Comparato positions its service as genuinely independent and customer-first — advisor recommendations are not influenced by referral fee amounts, and the company operates under a formal compliance framework as a signatory to the Private Health Insurance Intermediaries (PHII) Code of Conduct.

## Commercial & Legal Approach

- Engagement Model:
    - Comparato's service is free and no-obligation for consumers. Customers pay no fees and premiums are not marked up in any way — the price is identical to buying directly from a health fund.
    - Comparato earns a referral fee from the health fund the customer ultimately selects, paid via the ItsMy Group. This fee is not disclosed to advisors and does not influence recommendations.
- Contracts:
    - No contracts or lock-in for consumers. Health insurance policies are governed by the individual fund's terms and conditions, not Comparato's.
    - Customers may cancel their policy at any time in accordance with standard private health insurance cooling-off provisions (typically 30 days from commencement).
- Compliance:
    - Health insurance advice is provided by Comparato Pty Ltd and is a signatory to the Private Health Insurance Intermediaries (PHII) Code of Conduct.
    - Comparato holds certification with the Private Health Insurance Association (PHIA).
    - All customer calls are recorded and quality-assessed using a multi-chain AI compliance architecture against insurer-specific requirements.
